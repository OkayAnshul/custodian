# Second pass over the benign-divergence labels.
#
# The governing distinction, which the first drafts got wrong in four places:
# under this system REJECT means a constraint failed or the two things are
# unrelated. HOLD means "plausible, and a human decides". A bad-but-plausible
# substitution between related ingredients is what HOLD is for — a shopkeeper
# out of atta asks, they do not refuse.

# ── coconut milk ↔ coconut cream ────────────────────────────────────────────
# Cream is coconut milk with less water. Thin it and it is the same thing;
# every South Indian and Thai kitchen treats them as interchangeable.
benign-001: APPROVE
benign-002: APPROVE
benign-oos-001: APPROVE
benign-oos-002: APPROVE

# ── coconut milk → coconut milk powder ──────────────────────────────────────
# Reconstitutes correctly and is sold for exactly this. But the buyer does the
# work, and someone cooking tonight expecting a tin gets a packet. Ask.
benign-003: HOLD
benign-oos-003: HOLD

# ── coriander whole ↔ powder ────────────────────────────────────────────────
# Whole seeds are for tadka, where powder burns. Powder is for masala, where
# seeds are wrong. Which one matters depends entirely on the dish.
benign-004: HOLD
benign-005: HOLD
benign-oos-004: HOLD
benign-oos-005: HOLD

# ── turmeric whole → powder ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
# Powder is the dominant form, so this is usually fine. But someone who says
# "whole turmeric" may want the root — for pickle or juice — and powder is
# useless there. Genuinely underspecified.
benign-006: HOLD
benign-oos-006: HOLD

# ── mustard seeds → mustard oil ─────────────────────────────────────────────
# The worst substitution in this set. Same plant, entirely different ingredient:
# you cannot temper with oil the way seeds pop in it. I drafted HOLD, argued
# myself to REJECT, and came back to HOLD for consistency with atta→maida below
# — under this system REJECT is not a quality verdict. See DECISIONS ADR-028:
# a same-base, incompatible-form swap arguably should be able to reject, and
# currently cannot. That is a real gap and this case is the evidence for it.
benign-007: HOLD
benign-oos-007: HOLD

# ── sunflower oil → groundnut oil ───────────────────────────────────────────
# Both neutral cooking oils, interchangeable for most Indian cooking, and the
# human said EQUIVALENT. Approving.
#
# LOWEST CONFIDENCE IN THE SET. Groundnut is peanut. Swapping in an allergen
# without flagging it is something a careful merchant would not do, and a
# reviewer could reasonably call this HOLD on safety rather than culinary
# grounds. The system has no allergen concept, which is the actual gap.
benign-008: APPROVE
benign-oos-008: APPROVE

# Same swap under SAME_BASE. The policy is the human's instruction, not the
# gate's opinion, and it forbids a base change however good the substitute is.
benign-009: REJECT
benign-oos-009: REJECT

# ── ghee → butter ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# Widely substituted. Butter carries water and milk solids, so it burns where
# ghee would not. Fine for most uses, wrong for high-heat tempering.
benign-010: HOLD
benign-oos-010: HOLD

# ── atta → maida ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# CHANGED from the draft's REJECT. Rotis made with maida are not rotis, and I
# labelled that as a refusal. But a shopkeeper out of atta asks rather than
# refuses, and refusing takes the decision away from someone who may want maida.
# REJECT here would be a quality judgment wearing a constraint's clothes.
benign-011: HOLD
benign-oos-011: HOLD

# ── dahi → fresh cream ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# Cultured and sour versus sweet and not. Wrong for raita, sometimes fine in a
# curry. Ask.
benign-012: HOLD
benign-oos-012: HOLD

# ── desiccated coconut → coconut powder ─────────────────────────────────────
# Both dried coconut; powder is finer. Interchangeable for most things, and the
# texture difference is real in a laddoo or barfi. The form pair is unlisted, so
# it escalates — and it is a good candidate for a table entry around 7500.
benign-013: HOLD
benign-oos-013: HOLD

# ── whole almonds → almond milk ─────────────────────────────────────────────
# Badam for garnish, soaking or grinding, against a carton of milk. Same base,
# and the mirror of the mustard case: base identity is necessary and nowhere
# near sufficient.
benign-014: HOLD
benign-oos-014: HOLD

# ── chana dal → moong dal ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
# CHANGED from the draft's REJECT, for the same reason as atta→maida. Different
# pulses, roughly double the cooking time, materially different dish — and still
# a question a shopkeeper would ask rather than answer for you.
benign-015: HOLD
benign-oos-015: HOLD
