# Human review of the 30 benign-divergence labels.
#
# Reviewed by OkayAnshul on 2026-09-03, case by case, against the evidence in
# REVIEW.md: the request, the offered item, both attribute placements, the table
# scores, and what the gate does today.
#
# The governing question, settled first because it decides several cases at once:
# REJECT means a hard constraint failed or the two items have no relationship.
# Anything related but possibly wrong is HOLD — a shopkeeper out of atta offers
# maida and lets the customer decide; they do not refuse the sale. That is a
# decision about what this system's three outcomes mean, and it closes ADR-028
# in favour of the current design: a substitution cannot be rejected on its
# merits, only on a constraint.
#
# Consequence, stated so it is not discovered later: the only REJECT in this
# class is 009, where the base changed under a SAME_BASE policy. Every judgment
# about cooking lands on APPROVE or HOLD.
#
# Each case is judged once and applied to both variants. The pair differs only
# in the goal prose ("for tonight's cooking" vs "or the closest thing you can
# get") and in the DEV/TEST split; the policy and the items are identical, and
# the gate never sees the prose. A split label inside a pair would be a claim
# that the gate is wrong, not a judgment about food.

# ── coconut milk ↔ coconut cream ────────────────────────────────────────────
# Cream is coconut milk with less water. Thin it and it is the same thing;
# every South Indian and Thai kitchen treats them as interchangeable, and a
# shopkeeper would swap it without asking.
benign-001: APPROVE
benign-002: APPROVE
benign-oos-001: APPROVE
benign-oos-002: APPROVE

# ── coconut milk → coconut milk powder ──────────────────────────────────────
# Reconstitutes correctly and is sold for exactly this. But the buyer does the
# work, and someone cooking tonight expecting a tin gets a packet. Ask.
benign-003: HOLD
benign-oos-003: HOLD

# ── coriander, both directions ──────────────────────────────────────────────
# Ground for whole is fine in a paste or masala and wrong for tempering, where
# the seed has to crackle. Whole for ground asks the buyer to grind. Both
# depend on the dish, which is what the escalation band is for.
benign-004: HOLD
benign-005: HOLD
benign-oos-004: HOLD
benign-oos-005: HOLD

# ── whole turmeric → turmeric powder ────────────────────────────────────────
# Powder is right for almost every dish and wrong for a pickle or anything
# wanting the root. Almost-always is not always, and the form pair is unlisted
# on purpose: guessing is the thing this system exists not to do.
benign-006: HOLD
benign-oos-006: HOLD

# ── mustard seeds → mustard oil ─────────────────────────────────────────────
# You cannot temper with oil. Same base is necessary and nowhere near
# sufficient — but the two are genuinely related, so it asks rather than
# refuses, consistent with the governing rule above.
benign-007: HOLD
benign-oos-007: HOLD

# ── sunflower oil → groundnut oil, under two policies ───────────────────────
# Under EQUIVALENT the human invited substitutes and both are neutral cooking
# oils at a listed equivalence: approve. Under SAME_BASE the base changed and
# the policy is the human's instruction, not the gate's opinion: reject. The
# same swap, two answers, and the difference is authority rather than cooking.
benign-008: APPROVE
benign-oos-008: APPROVE
benign-009: REJECT
benign-oos-009: REJECT

# ── ghee → butter ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# The most ordinary swap in an Indian kitchen, and still not automatic: butter
# carries water and milk solids and burns far earlier. Fine on a roti, wrong
# for frying.
benign-010: HOLD
benign-oos-010: HOLD

# ── atta → maida ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# Rotis made with maida are not rotis, and the equivalence is listed at 3000 to
# say so. Related, though — both wheat flour — so under the governing rule it
# asks. The first draft said REJECT; this is a considered HOLD rather than the
# second pass's inherited one.
benign-011: HOLD
benign-oos-011: HOLD

# ── dahi → fresh cream ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# Same dairy family, sourness absent. Works in a few dishes and ruins others.
benign-012: HOLD
benign-oos-012: HOLD

# ── desiccated coconut → coconut powder ─────────────────────────────────────
# Close, and the texture differs: fine in a chutney, wrong as a garnish or in a
# burfi. Form pair unlisted, so it escalates rather than being assumed.
benign-013: HOLD
benign-oos-013: HOLD

# ── almonds → almond milk ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
# Same base, wildly different use — the mirror of the coconut case, and the
# reason base identity alone is never treated as sufficient. Related enough to
# ask, not close enough to assume.
benign-014: HOLD
benign-oos-014: HOLD

# ── chana dal → moong dal ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
# Different pulses, different cooking times and textures, listed at 3000 so the
# low score is a stated judgment rather than an absent entry. The human invited
# substitutes, so it asks. Also a considered HOLD, not an inherited one.
benign-015: HOLD
benign-oos-015: HOLD
